# Awesome AI for Mass Spectrometry Papers

A curated collection of papers, software, datasets, and benchmarks at the intersection of artificial intelligence and mass spectrometry.

The collection focuses on **small-molecule mass spectrometry and metabolomics**. Contributions from the broader mass-spectrometry community are welcome when their AI relevance is clear.

Papers contributed by the maintainer are marked with ⭐.

Catalog metadata and the generated bibliography are dedicated under [CC0 1.0](LICENSE-DATA); repository automation and validation code are available under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
